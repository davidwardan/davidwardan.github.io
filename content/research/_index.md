> All of my research is integrated into the open-source packages **[cuTAGI](https://github.com/lhnguyen102/cuTAGI)** and **[Canari](https://github.com/Bayes-Works/canari)**, which I actively contribute to and help maintain.

My research focuses on making AI systems more reliable, adaptable, and aware of what they don't know.

### Core Areas

- 🧠 **Probabilistic Machine Learning**: Understanding uncertainty to build smarter, more reliable models.
- 🔄 **Online and Continual Learning**: Crafting AI systems that evaluate and adapt in real-time.
- 📈 **Global Modelling of Time Series**: Capturing the big picture in temporal data, from earthquakes to financial markets.
- 🌉 **Structural Health Monitoring**: Ensuring infrastructure safety through advanced algorithms.
