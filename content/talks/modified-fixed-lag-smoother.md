This seminar presented a modified version of the fixed-lag smoother, traditionally used to update states within a specific interval as new observations are received.

The proposed modification operates within a fixed-lag window and incorporates the RTS smoothing equation, enabling online smoothing in nonlinear settings such as neural networks.
