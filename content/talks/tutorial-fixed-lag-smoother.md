This seminar offered an introduction to the theory and practice of the fixed-lag smoother, a tool for online smoothing in time series analysis and signal processing.

The fixed-lag smoother operates on a defined interval of previous observations, providing a balance between incorporating historical data and remaining responsive to new information.
